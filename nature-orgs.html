Around Westman

These projects are ones which have given talks to us or taken us out on excursions. If you’re looking for volunteer opportunities, check out their websites!

Assiniboine Food Forest

assiniboinefoodforest.com

This year, AFFI is hosting their Annual General Meeting at Chez Angela! They’ll conduct the annual general business, including the election of board members, and chair of the board Dave Barnes will once again host a slide show of activities on the land.

Find more information on their Facebook page.

Bee City Brandon

beecitybrandon.com

We are dedicated to creating beautiful pollinator habitat and providing opportunities for Brandonites to learn about bees, other pollinators, and their conservation.

Brandon Riverbank

riverbank.mb.ca

The Riverbank Discovery Centre works in partnership with many community groups to serve Brandon and the Westman area as a cornerstone space to host educational and cultural initiatives, promote environmental stewardship and to foster healthy community relationships.

Friends of the Bluebirds

mbbluebirds.org

Friends of the Bluebirds was formed in 1975. This ever expanding dedicated group of volunteers continue the work of providing habitat. These multitudes of nesting boxes, called Bluebird Lines, are found all over southern Manitoba. These boxes provide seasonal homes to both the Mountain and Eastern Bluebird as well as the Tree Swallow.

Dan Chranowski from FOTB will be giving us a talk about their group in February!

Delta Marsh Bird Observatory

dmbo.ca

Delta Marsh Bird Observatory is hosting three virtual talks about birds in the upcoming months. The November talk will focus on Manitoba’s grassland bird species and their endangered habitat and December (lining up with Christmas Bird Counts) has a talk on birding through your kitchen window.

For more information on these talks and how to register, visit the DMBO website.

Manitoba Habitat Conservancy

mbhabitat.ca

Manitoba Habitat Conservancy is a Manitoba-based charitable organization dedicated to conservation, restoration, and enhancement of fish and wildlife habitat.

We’ve checked out their Lauder sandhills and Noble Lake locations on past outings!

Manitoba Important Bird Areas Program

importantbirdareasmb.ca

Manitoba is home to 36 Important Bird Areas that cover more than 15,680 km2. From the shores of Hudson Bay to the mixed-grass prairie in the southwest corner of the province, each IBA has its own significance to birds and biodiversity. Located on public and private land, many of Manitoba’s IBAs have no formal protection. Favourite birding destinations like Delta Marsh, Oak Hammock Marsh, Churchill, and Whitewater Lake all have IBA status.

Manitoba Burrowing Owl Recovery Program

mborp.ca

The Manitoba Burrowing Owl Recovery Program was founded by Alex Froese in 2013. The program is a non-profit, on-the-ground conservation program addressing the ongoing decline of endangered burrowing owls in southwestern Manitoba.

Across Canada

Birds Canada

birdscanada.org

At Birds Canada, we bring together the passion and dedication of thousands of volunteers and collaborators with the expertise of professional scientists to collect and share data, deepen our understanding of birds, and identify the conservation solutions needed for them to thrive.

Ducks Unlimited Canada

ducks.ca

We’re Canada’s leading science-based conservation organization. We conserve, restore and manage wetlands and associated habitats for waterfowl, other wildlife and people. Like you.

Nature Conservancy of Canada

natureconservancy.ca

Protecting nature takes bold action. For more than 60 years, we have brought supporters and partners together to protect and care for nature across Canada at an extraordinary scale. Now we’re accelerating conservation to do even more for nature and people.
